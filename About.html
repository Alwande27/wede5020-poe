About Premium Mobile Carwash

Professional car care brought directly to your home, workplace or preferred location.

Who We Are

Premium Mobile Carwash is a small business founded in 2024 in the northern part of KwaZulu-Natal, in a small town called Dundee.

We provide quick, professional and efficient carwash and detailing services. Whether you are at home, at work or anywhere in between, our team brings the carwash directly to you.

Our mobile carwash operates with a van pulling a trailer equipped with a water bowser. We bring our own equipment, including a generator, pressure washer, vacuum cleaner and foam gun.
